About

Easy NHK News adds a vocabulary list and English translations of every sentence to the daily NHK News Web Easy articles. You can read each article with furigana always on, shown when you tap a word, or switched off.

Coming later if there's interest: slowed-down versions of the NHK audio, Anki flashcards for the vocabulary, and EPUB editions of the articles (with the same vocab list and translations). If you'd like any of these, let me know on Reddit at r/LearnJapanese.

Easy NHK News is an independent learning project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NHK (Japan Broadcasting Corporation). All articles and audio are © NHK.
